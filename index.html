What it does

Finds every device

Scans your network and lists each phone, TV, speaker, camera, printer, console and smart bulb, with its address and maker.

Works out what it is

Uses the maker and the services a device offers to label it "Smart TV" or "Camera" instead of a string of numbers. You can rename anything.

Flags newcomers

A "Devices to review" list shows anything new, so you can tell a guest's phone from something you did not expect.

Spots risky settings

Security alerts warn about exposed services such as Remote Desktop or file sharing, with plain-English steps to fix them.

Rooms, floors and a map

Place devices in rooms and floors, drag them around, and export the map as a picture.

Speed tests and history

Run a speed test and keep the history, so you can show your provider when it dips.

Asset list and budget

Record owners, notes and purchase details, and see what is coming to the end of its life.

Reports and printing

Print or export a tidy inventory of everything on your network.

Works across your PCs

Run it on more than one PC and share one set of names and notes through a folder you control. Nothing is uploaded.

Smart-home plugins

Connect Philips Hue, Home Assistant, Hubitat, SmartThings, Homey, openHAB or HomeSeer so their devices show up in every scan.

Alerts to your phone and email

Get a message when something new joins or a device you care about goes offline, without having to open the app.

Who's home

Link devices to people and see at a glance who is home, based on whose phone is on the Wi-Fi.

Runs in the background

Can start with Windows and keep watch quietly, so changes are caught as they happen.

Private by design

No account, no cloud. Your device list stays on your own PCs.

Import what you have

Bring in existing names, rooms and owners from a spreadsheet instead of typing them again.

Questions

Is it available now?

Not yet. It works and is being tested on a few home PCs. The waitlist tells me how many people want it before I put it on sale.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Scan results are stored on your own PC, and shared between your PCs only through a folder you choose. Your email address here is used only to tell you about the launch.

Is Wirevo Pro the final name?

It is the working name and may change before launch. If it does, I will tell the people on the list.

Which Windows versions?

Windows 10 and 11.

Will you pass on my email address?

No. It is used only to send updates about Wirevo Pro, and every email will have an unsubscribe link.
